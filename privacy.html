Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

ComfyUI Video SNS Automation is designed to run locally on the creator's Windows computer. The tool uses TikTok Login Kit to identify the account the creator authorizes and, when configured, TikTok Content Posting API to upload a video selected by the creator as a draft. The creator can review and publish the draft in TikTok.

Account authorization data and video files are handled by the local workflow and TikTok. This website does not provide an account, collect analytics, or sell personal information. TikTok processes information under its own privacy policy and terms. The creator can revoke the app's access through TikTok's account settings.
